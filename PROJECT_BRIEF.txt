1. THE DESTINATION

Build an AI-powered, repeatable fundamental investment research system that can
cover many companies and eventually multiple sectors. It should collect evidence,
build and maintain economically coherent company models, generate defensible joint
forecast distributions, estimate fundamental value, investigate what current prices
require, assess catalysts, and improve its methods using recorded forward results.

Nestlé is the first implementation case. Success means proving an end-to-end
process that can be reused for another company without duplicating the research
engine. The architecture should support a shared engine, sector templates and
company configurations, while allowing real differences in business economics.

The user wants speed, substantial AI involvement and continuous improvement. Those
are product goals. They do not establish that a relationship is predictive or that
a probability is calibrated. Make the process fast through reuse, targeted updates
and numerical computation, then measure whether its forecasts improve.

The system should help answer:
- What could happen to this company's operating performance and cash flows?
- Which drivers, dependencies and uncertainties matter, and what supports them?
- What is the resulting range of estimated fundamental value?
- Which operating cases are consistent with today's price under stated assumptions?
- Where does our independent view differ from those cases or dated consensus?
- What evidence or event could resolve that disagreement, and over what horizon?
- What changed since the last run, why, and which research deserves attention next?
- Do the forecasts improve on simpler alternatives as real outcomes arrive?

Portfolio sizing, exit rules and broker orders are outside the current scope.
Do not quietly expand into portfolio management or live execution.

2. THE COMPLETE RESEARCH CYCLE

A. Collect and preserve original sources and their availability history.
B. Extract verifiable observations and qualitative claims, keeping interpretation
   separate from reported facts. Reconcile material accounting inputs.
C. Map evidence to shared economic factors and company-specific transmission.
D. Maintain versioned model equations and reasoned distribution specifications.
E. Generate coherent numerical paths and calculate financial and valuation outputs.
F. Explain the output and its changes, with an inspectable path back to evidence.
G. Record forecasts before their outcomes become public and evaluate them later.
H. Use weaknesses, sensitivity and forecast errors to propose and test improvements.

Two activities must stay distinct:
- Routine updates: incorporate new evidence through an existing, documented model
  and update method, then recalculate affected outputs.
- Research and improvement: discover or challenge relationships, propose alternative
  models/distributions and compare them with an incumbent before promotion.

Company initiation and maintenance use this same cycle. Initiation establishes a
source-backed reporting map, reconciled history, equations, initial assumptions and
the first reproducible output. Maintenance evaluates new evidence against that
baseline, records what changed or why no change was warranted, and recalculates
affected dependencies. Do not rebuild a company's model from scratch for each item
of news or let an update erase how an earlier conclusion was reached.

AI should progressively support onboarding, extraction, reconciliation proposals,
model construction, distribution design, evidence interpretation, relationship
discovery, review and explanation. Numerical code owns accounting, parameter fitting,
sampling, valuation, solver calculations and scoring. Do not call an LLM once per
simulation path or use prose reasoning as the arithmetic engine.

The user's goal is AI-assisted execution and challenge with little routine human
intervention. Proposed automation uses bounded proposal/review/revision cycles,
source checks and deterministic accounting checks, with explicit stop and acceptance
criteria. On failure retain the last valid result and record an exception. Agreement
between agents is not independent evidence of accuracy. Validate the process on
reviewed examples and forward outcomes before relying on unattended operation;
new forecast model structures remain candidates until a documented promotion gate.
Initial historical models may be accepted automatically by the bounded source,
accounting and review gates; they do not require a human to pilot each stage.

Research tasks should identify an economic mechanism, observable target, expected
lag, necessary data, competing explanation, baseline, cost and a way to reject the
hypothesis. Focus on material, uncertain assumptions where better evidence could
change the conclusion. More documents, agents or complexity are not success metrics.

3. REUSE EVIDENCE; MODEL COMPANY EFFECTS EXPLICITLY

Store common research once. Coffee, FX and later macro or commodity research can
inform multiple companies through the same underlying evidence and scenario state.
Different firms need different exposures, products, purchasing and reporting
currencies, hedges, contract timing, inventory lags and pricing responses.

For example, a coffee-price change may affect Nestlé and Keurig Dr Pepper, but its
magnitude and timing must follow each company's supported exposure mapping. Topic
relevance does not prove a quantified exposure. Undisclosed details remain explicit
estimates or data gaps. Keurig Dr Pepper is an example of possible future reuse,
not a completed model or a fixed promise about the next company. Its unused
configuration placeholder was removed during the 10 October 2026 cleanup.

FX is a shared factor used wherever relevant. Separate translation, transaction,
hedging and balance-sheet effects. Derive cross rates coherently from a common
currency state. Avoid counting the same FX effect twice when translating a commodity
cost and then translating the company's financial statements.

One shared scenario ID represents the same economic future across linked factors,
periods and companies within the same run. Company-specific residual risks can remain.
This consistency supports research comparison; it does not add portfolio sizing.

4. PROBABILITIES AND ECONOMICS

Keep observed facts, distribution specifications and actual sampled numerical paths
as different objects. A specification records what is uncertain, its support,
parameters, dependencies, persistence, evidence and version. The model consumes
numerical paths and derives dependent financial rows through accounting equations.

Do not independently randomize sales, costs, margins and profit. Do not ask AI to
invent precise probabilities for every spreadsheet cell. Start with a small set of
material drivers and preserve common shocks, parameter uncertainty and time lags.
Distinguish estimated, pooled, external and judgmental inputs. Qualitative evidence
without a credible likelihood may justify scenario sensitivity rather than a precise
posterior. Uncertainty can increase when new evidence challenges an existing model.

Respect each company's reporting definitions, fiscal periods, currency conventions
and observed disclosure frequency. A sector template must not force economically
different companies into identical equations. Missing observations are not zeros;
undisclosed historical quarters must not be manufactured and treated as reported.

5. DISTINCT OUTPUTS AND THEIR LIMITATIONS

Fundamental forecasts:
Joint distributions for observable operating results, cash flows and relevant KPIs.
These can be evaluated against subsequently disclosed outcomes.

Estimated fundamental value:
Uncertainty about value under explicit operating, reinvestment, discount, terminal
and equity-bridge assumptions. Preserve the distinction between future operating
shocks and uncertainty about conditional expected cash flows. Check that numerical
simulation error is not mistaken for economic uncertainty.

Reverse valuation:
Operating cases that reproduce the observed price under stated other assumptions.
Growth, margins, reinvestment and discount policy must remain consistent with the
forward valuation. A price does not uniquely identify the market's beliefs. Keep our
independent forecasts separate from price-fitted cases.

Catalyst analysis:
Events with observable disclosures, disputed drivers and explicit timing. Explore
what information they reveal and how candidate market expectations might change.
Separate an expectation revision from an actual traded-price response; include
no-information outcomes and unsupported assumptions visibly.

Forward performance:
A record of issued predictions, resolved outcomes, baselines, calibration and errors.
Keep fundamental accuracy, disclosure prediction and market response distinct.

Later conditional extensions:
Horizon-specific traded-price distributions and instrument payoff comparisons were
part of the earlier roadmap. They require their own market-response assumptions,
quote/contract conventions and evidence. A DCF value histogram is not an option-expiry
price distribution. Preserve these ambitions without making them immediate work.

6. WHAT MUST BE AUDITABLE AND REPRODUCIBLE

Preserve source identity, units, definitions, period, publication time, receipt time,
revisions and exact evidence references. Keep assumptions, equations, code and run
versions. Save actual samples, AI outputs and environment information when needed
for replay; a random seed or repeated prompt alone is insufficient.

Each run must use a coherent frozen input snapshot. Repeated reports of the same
event must not become independent evidence. Revisions must not erase earlier inputs.
Older jobs must not replace newer valid results. Failures should preserve the last
valid output with a clear status. Local hashes help traceability but do not by
themselves prove a tamper-resistant, pre-event forecasting record.

Separate ingestion frequency, assumption revision frequency and recalculation
frequency. Frequent FX observations need not trigger full AI research for every
company. Cache valid extraction, batch appropriate updates and recalculate affected
dependencies. Speed must be measured alongside correctness, freshness and cost.

7. LEARN THROUGH FORWARD EVIDENCE

A full historical replay of the evolving AI process is impractical as the primary
validation method. Use prospective evidence for the overall process, while retaining
historical component estimation, numerical validation and diagnostic checks with
their limitations explicit. Record forecasts before outcomes become public.

Define the target, reporting vintage, horizon and resolution rule before issuance.
Compare with simple baselines using the same available information. Assess distribution
scores, interval coverage and width as appropriate. Repeated daily forecasts of one
result are not independent outcomes. Source restatements must not rewrite the
original score. Share-price performance alone does not validate every DCF assumption.

Register candidate mechanisms and evaluation rules before new results. Retain failed
candidates and uncertainty about performance. Do not promote whichever of many
models recently looked best without accounting for that selection. Verified software
or accounting bugs can follow a targeted correction path; new predictive claims
need evidence. Research agents must not silently rewrite production models or history.

10. DELIVER SMALL COMPLETE STEPS THAT SERVE THE WHOLE SYSTEM

The immediate scope, corrected by the user on 10 October 2026, is reusable autonomous
historical initiation. A company query must trigger AI discovery of legal identity,
reporting structure and authoritative sources, followed by cited extraction,
independent challenge, deterministic checks and bounded revisions. Do not require
a bespoke company extractor or a user-prepared historical model. API credentials
and model choices are private local settings, separate from tracked code and frozen
research inputs. Record token usage and dated cost estimates alongside each run;
select models through measured accuracy/cost comparisons, not assumed agent agreement.

Retrieve original financial disclosures, preserve exact sources and reporting
versions, build and reconcile history, and provide an inspectable model with empty
forecast periods and documented accounting links. No forecast values, assumption
estimation, distributions or DCF belong in this step. Bounded AI orchestration for
this historical step is now explicitly authorized; earlier deferrals are superseded.
Numbers must come from archived source locations, never merely AI-generated amounts.
Batch extraction and let reviewers independently navigate sources. Record prompts,
responses, model identities, critiques, revisions and timestamps. End unresolved
runs as incomplete without asking a human to pilot each stage. Zero errors cannot
be guaranteed: source quality, model mistakes and correlated reviewer errors remain.

Initiation must preserve completed work through interruptions and resume compatible
progress on the next matching request, without repaying for saved AI responses.
Keep earlier runs and original sources intact, reconstruct independent review
contexts, and carry one cumulative token/cost history and spending allowance across
continuations. Freeze the research date and verify code, settings, parsers and
evidence before reuse; incompatible progress needs an explicit fresh start, never
an unnoticed paid restart. Record a pending request before sending it and preserve
returned usage. A lost response with an unknown charge cannot safely permit more
spending. Resuming an initiation is distinct from refreshing completed research
with new evidence or accepting unfinished checks.

Historical initiation must include reported quarterly and half-year disclosures,
as well as annuals, within the selected fiscal years. Preserve cumulative nine-month
figures separately from standalone quarters, with explicit fiscal dates and stock,
flow, ratio or average definitions. Respect sales-only releases rather than inventing
quarterly accounts. Missing disclosures need cited reasons and independent coverage
review. Overlapping durations must not be added together; any later cross-period
derivation needs compatible definitions and reporting vintages and must be labelled
calculated. Cash continuity follows actual balance dates, not column position.

The user authorized three complementary check sets: deterministic reconciliation,
blind source reconstruction, and an evidence-first completeness/structure review.
Record independent facts and expected disclosures before revealing the proposed
answers; compare meanings and amounts afterward, without using peer approval as
evidence. Rerun checks after revisions and preserve disagreements. Permit documented
reporting differences and period-specific adjustments instead of forcing balances
or inventing historical zeros. Separate reviewer contexts improve the process but
do not establish statistically independent errors or a numerical confidence level.

13. IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARIES

Consolidated from the former architecture document during the 10 October 2026
cleanup. These are implementation principles and proposals, not built capabilities.

Operating model and valuation:
- Start with disclosed operating segments, then reconcile corporate/unallocated
  items into the group income statement. Link the balance sheet and cash-flow
  statement through working capital, investment/depreciation, cash, debt, interest,
  leases, equity movements and share-count schedules as applicable. Preserve both
  historical observations and future periods; distinguish reported, calculated,
  estimated and missing values. Segment definitions and restatements need versions.
- Do not fabricate undisclosed segment balance sheets or add overlapping product
  and geographic sales views together. Each scenario must satisfy accounting and
  cash/debt identities, with an explicit financing convention rather than arbitrary
  balancing entries. Use fundamental drivers at a granularity the evidence supports.
- Keep the DCF separate from operating equations. Its purpose is to translate
  coherent operating cash flows into estimated fundamental value per share, not
  create operating forecasts or establish a distribution of future traded prices.
  A reusable dcf.py is proposed when the calculation is implemented. Explicit
  policies must connect operating cash flow to valuation cash flow, discount and
  terminal assumptions, and the enterprise-to-equity bridge without double counting.
- Proposed model.xlsx and dcf.xlsx exports would share a run and frozen inputs;
  Python remains the calculation authority. Expose sources, assumptions, calculations
  and checks without fragile links to another editable workbook. Show a selected
  coherent scenario separately from distribution summaries: independent row medians
  need not form a balanced financial statement.

Code and storage:
- Keep the package flat and small. Separate responsibilities within cohesive code;
  a future capability does not automatically justify a module or directory now.
  README owns which components are written, executed and verified. The historical
  initiation step should not recreate the deleted full-platform scaffolding.
- Company-specific reporting definitions and source mappings belong in versioned
  initiation results, separate from reusable API, source and workflow code. Do not
  force company differences into one generic formula. Unsupported economics or
  missing disclosures remain explicit gaps rather than invented template inputs.
- Initiation must not require a pre-existing company configuration. The obsolete
  setup configs and synthetic FX demo were removed to keep the active code focused.
  Discovered identity and reporting definitions are frozen in the run. A later maintained baseline needs explicit versioning and
  promotion rules. Evolving estimates and observations are not editable equations.
- A proposed later local_store/research.sqlite design holds source identity/availability, extracted
  facts, distribution versions and company mappings in distinct records. Original
  documents belong in local_store/raw_sources/. Use one storage-access boundary;
  financial equations must not query storage directly. Create these only when needed.
- Distribution records identify driver, shared/company scope, units, period coverage,
  family/representation, parameters and support, dependency/persistence specification,
  evidence, estimation method, judgment/calibration status, version and availability.
  A company exposure is separate from the distribution of a shared factor.
- Link every material assumption revision to the evidence, calculation or qualitative
  interpretation supporting it, the previous version and the estimation/update method.
  Preserve decisions to retain, reject or defer an update as well as accepted changes.
  When AI is introduced, record prompts, model identifiers, outputs and review results
  alongside their input versions. Store equation/method versions once and reference
  them from calculations; do not duplicate editable formulas throughout the database.
- Select explicit versions using a coherent snapshot and information cutoff, validate
  definitions/currencies/periods, then freeze inputs. Missing material assumptions
  remain gaps or errors; do not invent defaults or fetch newer inputs during a run.
- Sampling code supplies joint numerical driver paths to equations in memory.
  Financial equations receive historical starting values, mappings and paths; they
  neither draw random values for dependent financial rows nor read editable Excel cells.
- Preserve input snapshots, actual samples, financial outputs, checks, code/environment
  provenance and the future review workbook under outputs/<run_id>/. Shared samples
  are stored once per run. Retain array axes, units, scenario IDs and period labels.
  For example, FX can be indexed as fx[scenario, period, currency].
- Frozen run exports preserve history, rather than providing another editable assumption
  store. Replay from frozen inputs/samples, not today's database. A random seed or
  source hash alone cannot recreate unavailable code, data or environment versions.
- Excel will show material source-linked assumptions, calculations and checks. It is
  a review interface; importing future edits requires an explicit validation contract.
- Keep database/run artifacts out of Git and back them up separately. Production
  storage requires appropriate migrations, transactions and concurrency controls;
  shared infrastructure follows demonstrated need rather than a predetermined stack.

FX mechanics:
- Store base_currency, quote_currency and quote_per_base explicitly. Define q_C as
  currency C units per USD, so units of B per A equal q_B / q_A. The computational
  reference does not change the company's reporting currency.
- Observed quotes need compatible timestamps and conventions. Keep original quotes
  and flag conflicts; never silently alter observations to make them reconcile.
- Derive daily cross rates before appropriate period aggregation. A ratio of two
  period-average currency levels is generally not the average of their daily cross rates.
- Use period-average rates only when appropriate for flows, closing rates for relevant
  balance-sheet claims, and explicitly dated rates for present valuation conversion.
  Reconcile the company's disclosed FX bridge and align currency and discount policy.
- Distinguish transaction, translation, hedging, purchasing/functional currencies,
  inventory/contract lags and balance-sheet effects. Convert a commodity into its
  purchase currency once, apply timing, then translate statements without double counting.
  Commodity types, grades and maturities require distinct definitions.
- These are requirements for future economic modelling. The removed single-period
  synthetic demo did not implement these accounting schedules; saved demo runs remain historical examples.

Evidence updates:
- Preserve source content hashes, publication/receipt times and exact fact passages;
  missing publication times remain unknown. Link repeated reports of the same event.
- Route validated facts to affected factors and assumptions, propose a versioned
  change with its mechanism/limitations, apply the documented update rule and save
  a new checked run. Advance current results only if expected input versions still match.
- Cache extraction by document/parser version, batch observations and separate
  collection, assumption revision and calculation frequencies. Invoke AI when evidence
  needs interpretation rather than once per tick, company or simulation path.
- Start manually. Scheduling follows idempotent reruns, versioned inputs and preservation
  of the last valid result on failure. Research into alternative equations stays separate
  from routine updates; additional complexity must earn its place through measured benefit.
